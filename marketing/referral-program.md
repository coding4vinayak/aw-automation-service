1. **Refer a business** to AutoFlow
2. **They become a client** (purchase any package)

## FAQ

**Q: How do I track my referrals?**
A: Email us anytime with "Referral status" and we'll send you an update.

**Q: When do I get my credit?**
A: Credit is applied within 7 days of your referral's payment clearing.

**Q: Can I use credit for any service?**
A: Yes! Credits can be applied to any package or service.

**Q: Is there a limit?**
A: No limit! Refer as many businesses as you want.

**Q: What if my referral doesn't become a client?**
A: No reward, but we appreciate the introduction anyway!
