# Cold Email Templates for Automation Services

## Template 1: Problem-Aware Cold Email

```
Subject: I noticed [specific issue] at [Business Name]

Hi [Name],

I was looking at your website and noticed [specific issue: no lead capture form / no appointment booking / no follow-up system].

This is costing you money because:
- [Specific problem: "Leads are going cold without follow-up"]
- [Quantified impact: "That's ~$X lost revenue per month"]

I built a free automation that fixes this. Takes 24 hours to set up.

Want me to send you a 2-min video showing how it works?

No strings attached.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

- Can I help [Business Name] get more leads?

---

## Best Practices

1. **Personalize** - Mention their business name and specific issue
2. **Keep it short** - Under 150 words
3. **One CTA** - Don't overwhelm with options
4. **Follow up** - 3 touches over 7 days
5. **Provide value** - Free sample/demo works best
